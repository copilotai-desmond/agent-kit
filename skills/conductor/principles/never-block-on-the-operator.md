# Never block on the operator

**When:** Tempted to ask the operator about reversible work.

**Why:** A question stops all progress until someone answers. Most questions can be settled by looking.

SKILL.md sets what proceeds and what needs a pause. This adds how to ask and what to do meanwhile.

- When you ask, bring options, your recommendation and what each costs. Never ask an open question.
- While waiting, keep going on work the answer does not touch.
- When the operator says to keep going, note each assumption you made in the reply.
- Pick the safer default, record it, and make it easy to undo.

**Example.** Two config names are possible and both work. Choose one, note it in the reply, and move on.
